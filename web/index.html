HOW TO CONNECT

Everyone plays through our server in Finland. Works from anywhere; the lower the ping, the smoother the game.

Your device connects straight to your friends' devices, with no server in between. Usually smoother, especially if you're far from Finland.

  • Works best when everyone is on the same Wi-Fi
  • Turn off your VPN
  • Hosting? Keep the game open on screen: your device runs the match

Phone hotspots don't work for direct play: the phone sharing its hotspot can't connect to the phones on it. Use a Wi-Fi router, or the Finland server.

A room showing CAN'T CONNECT means your network blocks direct play. Use the Finland server instead.
